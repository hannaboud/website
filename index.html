Working Paper · 2026
Rare Earth Price Risk in U.S. Manufacturing

Do stock markets correctly price rare earth supply chain risk? We find that they don't — the standard equity-based index has a daily correlation of ρ = 0.02 with actual metal prices. Evidence from 29 S&P 500 manufacturers using GJR-GARCH filtered quantile-on-quantile regression.

Coursework · 2026
AAPL Covered-Call Backtest

A weekly covered-call strategy backtested on real LSEG tape: strike chosen by targeting a Black-Scholes delta that shifts with the implied-vol regime and pulls in further after a losing week. Full Reg T margin accounting and a 6,500+ point mid-vs-trade validation (R² = 0.999) justify the fill assumption.
