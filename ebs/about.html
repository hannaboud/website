Who we are

A Duke student organization connecting students with international business since 2024.

Our mission

Inside Duke, EBS gives its members hands-on exposure to international business through applied consulting work.

Outside Duke, EBS delivers research and advisory services to its clients: US market expansion, site selection analysis, pitch deck development, translations and strategic consulting across many sectors.

Faculty support

Our faculty advisors come from Duke Economics and the Fuqua School of Business. They review our methods and help project teams when the questions get hard.
