Membership

Open to Duke students of every major who want real consulting experience.

What you'll do

You join a small project team with a project lead and work for one client through the semester: researching, analyzing, building the deliverable and presenting it.

What you'll learn

How to structure a business problem, do market research that holds up, work with a client, and present to people who will act on what you say.

How to join

  1. Come to an info session

    Attending one is required to take part in rush.

  2. Apply

    Submit the application before the deadline.

  3. Interview

    Selected applicants meet members of the team.

  4. Start your first project

    New members are trained and placed on a project team.
